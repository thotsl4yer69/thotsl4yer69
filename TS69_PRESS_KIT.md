**Tagline:** GOOD TROUBLE. MADE REAL.

**Short biography:** THOTSL4YER69 INDUSTRIES — TS69 for short — operates at the intersection of internet culture and things you can actually play, wear and use. ## Usage and factual boundaries

TS69 Industries is a **creative brand identity**, not a claim of registered corporate status, a registered trade mark or an established workforce. Retail purchases are processed and fulfilled by the linked third-party marketplace, subject to its stock and pricing. **JUST THE TIP** is a public development project; do not claim a Steam/Play Store commercial launch or production-ready status without evidence. The mature game is kept separate from all-ages graphics and maker content where required.
