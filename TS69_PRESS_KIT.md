## Usage and factual boundaries

TS69 Industries is a **creative brand identity**, not a claim of registered corporate status, a registered trade mark or an established workforce. Retail purchases are processed and fulfilled by the linked third-party marketplace, subject to its stock and pricing. **JUST THE TIP** is a secondary experimental project awaiting owner acceptance; do not market it as the company's flagship, claim a Steam/Play Store release or misrepresent its maturity. The mature game is kept separate from all-ages graphics and maker content where required.
